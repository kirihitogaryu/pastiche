Dutch Angle

Aliases: canted_angle, dutch_tilt, oblique_angle

A composition where the scene’s horizon, ground plane, or dominant spatial axis is deliberately tilted relative to the frame, creating an unstable, disorienting, or canted effect.

  • effect: unstable, dramatic, disorienting, energetic

Tagging guidance

Use this tag when the dominant spatial axes of the scene are visibly canted relative to the frame. If tilting your head makes the image feel more “level,” this tag is probably appropriate.

Do not use this tag for a single diagonal object, a leaning figure, a sloped hill, or ordinary perspective lines. In those cases, use diagonal_composition, dynamic_pose, or tilted_horizon instead.

AI tagging guidance

Agents may suggest dutch_angle when multiple architectural, horizon, or ground-plane cues indicate that the scene itself is tilted. Agents should not apply the tag automatically when only a character pose or isolated object is diagonal.
